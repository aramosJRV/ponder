**Signing gotcha:** `android/app/build.gradle` has **no `signingConfigs`
block** and there's no keystore in the repo — every previous AAB (codes 5–9)
was signed through Android Studio's dialog. `./gradlew bundleRelease` alone
produces an *unsigned* bundle that Play will reject. Use:

> Android Studio → **Build → Generate Signed App Bundle / APK → Android App
> Bundle**, pick your existing upload keystore, `release` variant.
