### 2.3 Verse validation fails ~18% of the time, and the cost model ignores it

Per-day, Aug 1–9 (6 entries/day, all-Sonnet — this predates the Haiku routing):

| Day | Entries | attempt_1 fail | attempt_2 fail | fallback used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 08-09 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 08-08 | 6 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 08-07 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 08-06 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 08-05 | 5 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| 08-04 | 6 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| 08-03 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 08-02 | 6 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 08-01 | 6 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Roughly **1 in 6 entries fails verse validation on the first attempt**, and on
2 of 9 days the curated fallback verse was served instead of anything the model
chose.

Two consequences:

1. **The cost table assumes one model call per entry.** It isn't one. ~18% of
   entries cost a second call, and the retry deliberately escalates to Sonnet —
   the expensive model. The real per-entry cost is above the quoted `$0.00825`.
2. **Haiku now writes ~75% of entries.** Citing a scripture reference that
   resolves to a real row is precisely the kind of task a smaller model does
   worse. Expect this rate to go up, not down. The Haiku A/B in the open-items
   list should measure *reference resolution rate*, not just prose quality.

### 2.5 Android `cap sync` has never run since RevenueCat was added

`android/capacitor.settings.gradle` includes only:


No RevenueCat. The iOS Podfile has it; Android does not. The first AAB would

**Add `npx cap sync android` to the front of the Android chain**, and check
that file again before building.

### 2.6 `'expired'` batch status is dead code

`generation_batches.status` permits `'expired'` in its CHECK constraint.
Nothing anywhere writes it — not the migration, not the edge function.

`collectBatches()` polls `.eq("status","submitted").limit(5)` ordered oldest
first. A batch that never reaches `ended` stays `submitted` **forever** and
permanently occupies one of those five slots. Five stuck batches and
collection stops completely, silently, with no alert.

Low probability, unbounded consequence. Needs a reaper: mark `expired` after
some age and stop polling it.

### 2.7 `batch_lead_hours() = 2` against a 24-hour SLA

The migration comment acknowledges the batch API is only guaranteed within 24h
and picks a 2-hour lead anyway. The degradation path exists — the Today screen
offers manual generation — but **the notification is the product**. A late
batch means a notification that announces an entry that isn't there, or no
notification at all.

Worth deciding deliberately. Options: longer lead, or generate the notification
body only after the entry lands.

### 2.8 Six active topics against a cap of three

`topics` where `status='active'` = **6**. `max_active_topics()` = 3.

Not a bug — `guard_active_topic_cap()` only fires on transitions *into* active,
which is documented and deliberate. But the existing seed/demo data is over cap
and will behave inconsistently during screenshot capture and testing.
