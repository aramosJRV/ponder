## What I actually looked at

| Account | Size | What they post |
|---|---|---|
| @youversion | 3.6M | Bold typographic verse graphics, direct question prompts ("Are you unsure about something in your life?"), talking-head reels |
| @hallowapp | 1.4M | Classical sacred art with text overlays, talking-head reels with burned-in captions |
| @shereadstruth | 915K | Tactile lifestyle photography — journals, books, coffee, hands, muted warm palette. Almost no typography-on-gradient |

Plus format research for faith accounts: **Reels are the reach engine** (the algorithm pushes them to non-followers), **carousels are the save engine** (people bookmark them and come back). Recommended mix for growth is roughly **3 reels : 1 carousel per week**.

## The honest read

Two of those three are billion-dollar-adjacent brands with studios. The third wins on photography you don't have. Copying any of them at 0 followers produces a fourth pretty-verse-graphic account in a category that has thousands, and pretty verse graphics are the single most commoditised content on Instagram.

**The thing you have that none of them have is the challenge entry.** Every app in this category is built to affirm. Ponder is built to occasionally tell the user they might be wrong about what they think God is saying. That is a genuinely contrarian position in this niche, it is emotionally interesting, and it is *arguable* — which is what makes people comment and share.

So the content strategy is not "daily verse". It is **discernment** — the hard question of how you tell the difference between God and your own wanting. Scripture graphics are the wallpaper; discernment is the reason to follow.

## Positioning line to run everywhere

> A devotional that asks harder questions.

## Account fixes before posting anything

1. **There is no link in the bio.** This is the entire point of the account. Add the App Store / Play links (or one landing page carrying both).
2. **The Name field is searched by Instagram; the handle is not, much.** It currently reads "Ponder: Daily Discernment". Change to something carrying the search terms: **`Ponder · Prayer Journal & Devotional`**.
3. Bio copy is good as-is. Keep "the things you're actually praying about" — that's the differentiator in plain words.

**Slides:**
1. How to keep a prayer journal you'll actually re-read
2. One notebook, many subjects = unreadable. You'll never find anything again.

## Cadence

4 posts a week, roughly 3 reels to 1 carousel. That's three weeks of content above.

Post reels Tue/Thu/Sun, carousel Saturday. Reply to every comment for the first three months — early comment velocity is most of what the algorithm has to go on for a new account.

## What to measure

Ignore likes. Watch **saves** on carousels and **shares** on reels — those two are what actually drive distribution, and they're the ones a small account can move. Follower count will lag both by weeks.
