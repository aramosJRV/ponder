## 1. The single biggest problem is not copy

**Ponder has too few ratings for the App Store to display a rating overview.**

Apple's search ranking is roughly keyword relevance x popularity, and rating
count/volume is the dominant popularity input for a small app. With effectively
zero ratings, no keyword rewrite will move you onto page one for anything
contested. Copy work below is worth doing — it's cheap and permanent — but it is
the second lever, not the first.

Action (code, not metadata): fire `SKStoreReviewController` / the Capacitor
in-app-review plugin at the right moment. Not on launch. The right moment for
this app is **immediately after the user saves their first note**, or after
reading their 3rd entry — a point where the app has just demonstrably worked.
Apple allows 3 prompts per 365 days per user; spend them deliberately.

At 134 installs / DAU 12, a prompt converting at even 5% gets you off zero,
which is the step-change. Going from 0 to 10 ratings matters more than 10 to 100.

## 2. Apple's indexed surface is 160 characters, and ~34 of yours are wasted

Apple indexes **name (30) + subtitle (30) + keyword field (100)** and
auto-recombines individual words into phrases. It also handles singular/plural
variants. Therefore repeating any word already in the name or subtitle inside the
keyword field earns **zero** additional ranking.

Current keyword field (101 chars — over Apple's 100 limit, so it would be
rejected or silently truncated as-is):

```
devotion,quiet,time,prayer,christian,faith,scripture,verse,reflect,discern,journaling,spiritual,daily
```

Dead weight:

| Term | Chars | Why it's wasted |
|---|---|---|
| `devotion` | 9 | "devotionals" is in the subtitle; plural matching covers it |
| `journaling` | 11 | "journal" is in the subtitle |
| `discern` | 8 | "Discernment" is in the app name |
| `daily` | 6 | "Daily" is in the app name |

That is 34 of 101 characters — a third of the field — buying nothing.

### Recommended (maximum coverage)

Subtitle (30/30) — moves `prayer` out of the keyword field and into an indexed,
human-readable slot:

```
Bible devotions prayer journal
```

Keyword field (100/100):

```
quiet,time,christian,faith,scripture,verse,reflect,spiritual,god,hearing,notes,study,guidance,psalms
```

Tradeoff, stated honestly: that subtitle reads more like keywords than like a
sentence. Reviewers allow it, but it's a conversion-vs-discovery trade.

### Conservative alternative (keeps the subtitle as-is)

Subtitle unchanged: `Bible devotionals & journal`

Keyword field (100/100):

```
quiet,time,prayer,christian,faith,scripture,verse,reflect,spiritual,god,hearing,notes,study,guidance
```

### What the new terms buy

Apple recombines, so adding `god` + `hearing` + `guidance` alone generates
coverage for *hearing god*, *god guidance*, *spiritual guidance*, *hearing god
daily*, *christian guidance*. That cluster is Ponder's actual differentiator and
nothing in the current field reaches it.

### Deliberately NOT included

- `plan` — "bible reading plan" is enormous and owned outright by YouVersion.
  Ponder is not a reading plan; you'd lose the ranking and mismatch the intent.
- `devotional`/`bible`/`journal`/`daily`/`discernment` — already indexed.
- `meditation` — higher volume, wrong audience. Draws mindfulness searchers who
  will bounce, and bounce hurts you.
- Competitor brand names (YouVersion, Hallow, Glorify, Abide) — Apple rejects
  trademarked terms and it holds up review.
- `app`, `free` — `app` matches without being listed.

## 3. Category: test Reference as primary

Currently Lifestyle (primary) / Reference (secondary).

YouVersion's Bible app — the category leader and the app your users already have
installed — sits in **Reference**. Lifestyle is an enormous generic bucket you
will never chart in. Reference is both narrower and where people browsing for
scripture apps actually look.

Recommend swapping: **Reference primary, Lifestyle secondary.** Low risk,
reversible, costs nothing.

## 4. Developer name inconsistency

The App Store page shows the developer as **Antonio Ramos**. The listing metadata
and copyright line say **JRV Solutions**. For a faith app asking people to write
private spiritual reflections, a personal name may actually convert better than a
company name — but pick one and make it consistent across the store page,
copyright, privacy policy and support page. Right now it reads as unsettled.

## 5. Promotional text is free, un-indexed, and editable without review

Current text is good and on-message. It is **not** indexed, so don't put keywords
there — it exists purely to convert. It is also the only field you can change
without shipping a build, which makes it the right place to test conversion
angles or seasonal hooks.

## 6. Google Play (unverified — check in console)

Play indexes **title + short description + full description**, with the short
description weighted heavily. There is no hidden keyword field, so keyword
density in the visible text is what ranks — the opposite of iOS.

Recorded short description (77/80):

```
Daily Bible devotionals for what you sense God is saying. Journal the pattern.
```

That is solid. Suggested alternative that adds `prayer journal` and `hearing`:

```
Bible devotions & prayer journal for hearing what God is saying to you.
```

For the full description, work the phrases *prayer journal*, *daily devotional*,
*hearing God*, *Bible study notes* in 3-5 times each, naturally. The current
description reuses the Apple copy, which is written for a store that does not
index descriptions at all — it under-performs on Play by design.
