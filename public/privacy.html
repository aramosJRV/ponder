Ponder — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

Ponder (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal daily devotional app published by JRV Solutions, based in Victoria, Australia. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep the app’s data footprint deliberately small.

Who is responsible for your data

JRV Solutions is the data controller for information processed through Ponder. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact us at antonio.ramos.jr@gmail.com.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, health data, financial information, or advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

How your content is used to generate devotional material

To prepare your daily entries and any on-demand syntheses, relevant parts of your content — such as a thread’s title, recent notes, and the scripture references already shown — are sent to a third-party artificial intelligence provider (Anthropic, which operates the Claude API) purely to generate reflection text. This processing happens on our servers, never directly from your device, and the resulting text is returned and stored with your entry. Your content is not used by that provider to train its models. Scripture text itself comes from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation stored in our own database, and is never generated or altered by the AI.

Where your data is stored

Your account and content are stored using Supabase, a hosted database and authentication provider, on infrastructure operated on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted to your own account through row-level security.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Supabase for storage and authentication, Anthropic for content generation) strictly to operate the app’s features.

Your choices and rights

You can view and delete your threads, notes, and journal entries within the app. You can request deletion of your entire account and all associated content by emailing antonio.ramos.jr@gmail.com; we will action deletion requests within a reasonable period. If you never provide an email address, your content remains tied only to the anonymous identifier on your device.

Children’s privacy

Ponder is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Reporting generated content

Because devotional text is generated by AI, the app includes a reporting control on every entry and synthesis. If content is offensive, inappropriate, or otherwise concerning, you can report it from within the app. Reports are stored so we can review and improve the experience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by the “last updated” date above and, where appropriate, communicated in the app.
